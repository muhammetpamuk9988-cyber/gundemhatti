GündemHattı – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

GündemHattı ("the App") is used only by its operator to publish news summary videos to the operator's own TikTok account, @gundemhatti.

Data we access

Through TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API, the App accesses only the operator's own TikTok account. The scopes it uses are user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish. Their only purpose is to upload videos and check that each upload succeeded.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer. They are never shared with or sold to third parties.

Viewers

The App does not collect any personal data from TikTok viewers.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. When access is revoked, the stored tokens are deleted.

Contact

Send a direct message to @gundemhatti on TikTok.
