GündemHattı – Terms of Service

Last updated: 14 November 2026

GündemHattı ("the App") is a personal content tool that prepares short Turkish news summary videos and publishes them only to the TikTok account @gundemhatti, which is owned by the App's operator.

1. Use of the App

The App is operated for a single account. It is not offered to the public, and no third-party TikTok accounts can sign in to it.

2. Content

Videos are news summaries based on publicly reported information. Each video names its source. Visuals come only from royalty-free or openly licensed sources (for example Pexels or Wikimedia Commons) and are credited. A human reviews every video before it is published.

3. TikTok

The App uses TikTok's official APIs and follows the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

4. Disclaimer

The content is provided for information only. Every effort is made to keep it accurate, but no guarantee is given. Correction requests can be sent through the contact address below.

5. Contact

Send a direct message to @gundemhatti on TikTok.
